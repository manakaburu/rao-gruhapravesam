# Approved Telugu and English copy deck

**Telugu poetic lines were composed collaboratively during the design discussion and approved as the working language direction.** These are *not* traditional shlokas; do not attribute them to religious or classical authors. Keep the precise Telugu Unicode lines from `docs/CONTENT_LOCK.json`, which takes precedence over this document if punctuation differs. A native Telugu editorial review remains advisable before publishing, but does not block build.

## Sacred entrance
`ముంగిట ముగ్గుల మురిపాలు…`
`గుమ్మాన తోరణాల సింగారాలు…`
`మా ఇంటికి మీ రాకే అసలైన సంబరాలు!`

English complementary meaning (not necessary to render under every line): The muggu and festive garlands make the entrance beautiful, but guests make it truly festive.

## Welcome interior
`కొత్త గోడలు… కొత్త కలలు…`
`మీ రాకతో మొదలయ్యే మధుర జ్ఞాపకాలు!`

English supporting invitation: The Rao's Family warmly invites you and your family to our new home celebration. Invited by Sri Sana Rambabu & Smt. Bhavani.

## Divine blessings
`ఏడు కొండలవాడి ఆశీస్సులతో…`
`మా కొత్త ఇంట శుభానికి శ్రీకారం…`
`మీ అందరి దీవెనలతో ఆనందాల హారం!`

English source-aligned: With the divine blessings of Lord Venkateswara and our beloved elders.

`విందు భోజనంతో మీతో గడపడం మాకు మరింత సంతోషం…`
`ఆప్యాయంగా వచ్చి, ఆనందంగా భోజనం చేసి ఆశీర్వదించండి!`

## Rendering and editorial boundaries
- Strings in main DOM text, not painted into image assets. `lang="te"` on poem blocks.
- Telugu punctuation/characters copied exactly, not reconstructed by typing approximate Latin or using machine translation.
- On small screens choose a slightly smaller typography scale rather than silently dropping lines.
- Devotional passage stays dignified; light playful tone belongs mainly in feast and closing sections.
- Do not invent alternate verses, extra rituals, a priest's name, deity idol details in real home, family biography, or additional event hosts.
